# Twitter AI - 2026-10-22

## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Agent standards move toward shared governance 🡕

[@ClaudeDevs](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs) [announced](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2113328526413996100) (320 likes, 28 replies, 15,590 views) that Anthropic is donating Agent Skills to the Agentic AI Foundation. The post says more than 70 agent platforms had integrated the standard since December 2025. Follow-up posts say existing licenses remain unchanged and identify versioning, a proposal process, and security guidance as early priorities.

**Discussion insight:** The follow-ups clarify that contribution does not require joining the foundation and that the steering committee is intended to include several model and agent developers. [@ClaudeDevs](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs) [outlined](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2113328528536305718) (86 likes, 2 replies, 5,510 views) the license and governance details.

### 1.2 Model-assisted formal verification reaches larger codebases 🡕

[@AnthropicAI](https://x.com/AnthropicAI) [reported](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2113390212911083894) (162 likes, 18 replies, 12,253 views) work with Theorem using Claude to prove selected software security properties across more than 100 open-source libraries. [The research post](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification) specifies 123 libraries and a bounded proof scope; it also publishes a subset of the proofs. This is evidence for a verification workflow, not a claim that all bugs in those projects were eliminated.

### 1.3 Procurement and workplace deployment put evaluation in context 🡒

[@murtuza_merc](https://x.com/murtuza_merc) [linked](https://x.com/murtuza_merc/status/2113305887007760485) (165 likes, 8 replies, 9,276 views) a report about Cohere’s Cultural Riddles benchmark and argued that schools may need region-specific chatbot validation. A second post [summarized](https://x.com/murtuza_merc/status/2113363564937216319) (139 likes, 9 replies, 9,473 views) Uber’s reported rollout of ChatGPT Work to more than 30,000 employees. The linked report describes research, analysis, and presentation workflows but does not report productivity outcomes.

**Comparison to prior day:** Yesterday’s posts described health and science agents; today the emphasis shifts to shared agent standards, formal verification, and the governance needed around deployment. [@EricTopol](https://x.com/EricTopol) [highlighted](https://x.com/EricTopol/status/2112935031329161534) (147 likes, 13 replies, 15,911 views) a multi-agent personal health research system on October 21.

## 2. What Frustrates People

### Evaluation must account for context and deployment

The school-procurement post connects chatbot reliability to region-specific validation, while the Uber rollout report identifies data controls and productivity measurement as implementation questions. These are concerns raised in linked commentary; the posts do not show measured procurement outcomes or realized productivity gains.

## 3. What People Wish Existed

### Culture-aware chatbot validation

The school-focused post explicitly calls for culture-aware benchmarks, and the linked report describes Cultural Riddles as a test of culture-bound reasoning. [@murtuza_merc](https://x.com/murtuza_merc) [made the case](https://x.com/murtuza_merc/status/2113305887007760485) (165 likes, 8 replies, 9,276 views) for region-specific validation. This is a practical, direct need for evaluation in multilingual or regional deployments; evidence of procurement demand remains a forecast in the post.
