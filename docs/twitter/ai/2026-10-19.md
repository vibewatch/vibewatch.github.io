# Twitter AI - 2026-10-19

## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Model-assisted security review 🡕

One prominent release applies frontier models to recurring open-source security scans. Anthropic describes OSS Scanner as a no-cost, opt-in service for critical projects; its site says the model-generated fast-track reports may not have had human review.

[@ClaudeDevs](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs) [announced](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2112299602510749992) (759 likes, 38 replies, 25,993 views) periodic scans with a proof of concept and a patch where possible. [The service page](https://red.anthropic.com/oss-scanner) says project maintainers enroll eligible repositories and receive reports directly.

### 1.2 Testing agent skills and exact image constraints 🡒

Two posts surface evaluation limits: one questions whether agent skills are actually useful, while another documents image models missing exact pixel dimensions. [@sermakarevich](https://x.com/sermakarevich) [asked](https://x.com/sermakarevich/status/2112198653469241856) (46 likes, 11 replies, 2,653 views) how to test a skill, arguing that many are untested and their value is subjective. In a reply, [@87Mohan87](https://x.com/87Mohan87) [cautioned](https://x.com/87Mohan87/status/2112232500290625959) (1 like, 1 reply, 9 views) that choosing benchmark tasks can be subjective too; the author agreed.

[@GrilliotTodd](https://x.com/GrilliotTodd) [compared](https://x.com/GrilliotTodd/status/2112267299378090314) (108 likes, 11 replies, 3,472 views) requested pixel-art sizes with the outputs from GPT Image 2.5, Nano Banana Pro, and Nano Banana 2. The image marks the requested canvas with dashed boxes and labels several model outputs at different sizes.

![Comparison of requested pixel-art dimensions and generated frog images from three image models](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HVBHxyibsAAnMv-.png)

**Comparison to prior day:** The October 18 discussion focused on harness benchmark gains and their added cost; today’s examples shift toward whether skills and model outputs can be reliably evaluated. [@PawelHuryn](https://x.com/PawelHuryn) [reported](https://x.com/PawelHuryn/status/2111730046691836064) (358 likes, 59 replies, 34,416 views) a 2–3 point gain from a model harness alongside 80% higher cost.

## 2. What Frustrates People

### Evaluation without dependable pass/fail criteria

The skill-testing post says there is rarely a clear pass/fail for whether a skill helped, while its reply notes that the test itself can reflect the evaluator’s subjective task selection. Separately, the pixel-art comparison shows a concrete mismatch between requested and generated dimensions. These are specific examples, not evidence of prevalence; the posts do not quantify downstream impact.

---

## 3. What People Wish Existed

### More objective skill and output checks

The skill author is explicitly looking for a way to test usefulness, and the image comparison illustrates a need for exact-size generation when dimensions matter. This is a practical, direct opportunity, but the discussion also warns that evaluation design can introduce its own subjectivity.
