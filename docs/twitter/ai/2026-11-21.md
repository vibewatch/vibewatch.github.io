## 2. What Frustrates People

### Agent process cleanup and shared-machine isolation

**Severity: High for the affected setup.** One reported login-route test left 14 Node processes running, consumed 18 GB of RAM, and changed a port setting. The author presents it as a harness failure; a practitioner reply says multiple agents share a machine and cleanup should not depend on agent behavior. This is one incident, not evidence of how often the problem occurs. The immediate workaround is to make the harness own task cleanup and isolate each run. **Worth building for:** agent runtimes that provide reliable process-tree cleanup and per-task isolation have a concrete engineering case.

[@PatchOnChain](https://x.com/PatchOnChain) [reported the incident](https://x.com/PatchOnChain/status/2123905243155193974) (10 likes, 21 replies, 664 views); [@OpenChurchlabs](https://x.com/OpenChurchlabs) [described the shared-machine concern](https://x.com/OpenChurchlabs/status/2123932812600606833) (4 views).

### Model comparisons can hide variant and measurement differences

**Severity: Medium.** In one same-codebase trial, the author called Gemini the overall winner but also noted a response-time figure that did not match end-to-end time. A reply asked that the exact Gemini variant be named. This is a small, single-run sample; the practical coping strategy in the thread is to specify variants, compare on the same task, and retain separate performance and test-quality measures. **Worth building for:** evaluation tooling that records exact model versions and measures wall-clock outcomes alongside generated tests.

[@bread_](https://x.com/bread_) [shared the comparison](https://x.com/bread_/status/2123908675110904160) (58 likes, 3 replies, 5,800 views, 18 bookmarks); [@OanaGoge](https://x.com/OanaGoge) [asked for variant-specific labeling](https://x.com/OanaGoge/status/2123920032946438196) (2 replies, 153 views).

### Disputed resource estimates for a proposed data centre

**Severity: Potentially high for nearby residents; confidence in the quoted figures is limited.** A post opposing a proposed 600 MW facility raises energy and water concerns, while a reply asks who calculated the estimates and whether energy would be generated onsite. The discussion does not resolve those questions. **Worth building for:** independently sourced, locally specific resource disclosures may help residents assess proposals; no product request was made in the thread.

[@JamesMelville](https://x.com/JamesMelville) [raised the local concerns](https://x.com/JamesMelville/status/2123935097686573222) (79 likes, 6 replies, 2,172 views); [@ctkulp](https://x.com/ctkulp) [challenged the sourcing](https://x.com/ctkulp/status/2123936059432087685) (1 like, 9 views).

## 3. What People Wish Existed

### A benchmark for human intervention in agent tasks

[@Aish_krrr](https://x.com/Aish_krrr) explicitly asks for a leaderboard measuring how many times a person must intervene before an agent finishes a job [post](https://x.com/Aish_krrr/status/2123924787059912794) (4 likes, 4 replies, 137 views, 1 bookmark). The post summarizes a video review, so it is evidence of the request, not independent evidence about the model's performance. This is a practical measurement need with moderate urgency for teams evaluating autonomous work. Existing run-count and CI proposals partially address repeatability, but the reviewed posts do not measure intervention burden. **Opportunity: Direct, with low-volume evidence today.**

### Human-rated communication and teaching quality

[@OutsourcedLogic](https://x.com/OutsourcedLogic) asks for communication benchmarks based on general-public ratings rather than automated scores [post](https://x.com/OutsourcedLogic/status/2123939297208447376) (1 reply, 19 views, 1 bookmark). In another thread, [@SnsblModerate](https://x.com/SnsblModerate) says they want an AI tutor that can teach with passable prose [reply](https://x.com/SnsblModerate/status/2123928489577623588) (4 likes, 1 reply, 248 views). Together these point to audience-fit writing and pedagogy as quality dimensions not captured by code-test results. The first is a practical evaluation request; the tutor request is aspirational, and the sample is small. **Opportunity: Direct for human-rated communication evaluation; aspirational for teaching products.**

## 6. New and Notable

### MedChemBench applies model evaluation to medicinal chemistry

[@BioAIFrontier](https://x.com/BioAIFrontier) reports that GPT-6.2 Astra earned 84.5% of available credit at xhigh on MedChemBench [post](https://x.com/BioAIFrontier/status/2123924450802593941) (9 likes, 1 reply, 388 views, 2 bookmarks). OpenAI's [announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-medchembench/) describes 99 problems drawn from 117 papers and patents, covering 761 molecular structures. The benchmark tests reading structures, interpreting experimental evidence, and selecting compounds; the score is not evidence that a model has discovered a drug or produced a laboratory result.

No results or comparative scores are included in the captured post.

## 8. Takeaways

1. **Treat model rankings as task-specific.** The Inkling launch posts use different loop-related parameter counts, while the same-codebase trial shows that a performance result can coexist with differences in test volume and timing. [@TeksEdge](https://x.com/TeksEdge) [posted task-level comparisons](https://x.com/TeksEdge/status/2123930642043793868) (37 likes, 4 replies, 2,301 views); [@bread_](https://x.com/bread_) [shared the codebase trial](https://x.com/bread_/status/2123908675110904160) (58 likes, 3 replies, 5,800 views).
2. **Agent reliability includes the harness.** Cleanup, isolation, and repeatable runs mattered as much as task completion in the process-leak post, the Argusic project run, and the proposed CI workflow. [@PatchOnChain](https://x.com/PatchOnChain) [reported a cleanup failure](https://x.com/PatchOnChain/status/2123905243155193974) (10 likes, 21 replies, 664 views); [@tranngocthuycom](https://x.com/tranngocthuycom) [reported clean-machine runs](https://x.com/tranngocthuycom/status/2123958502708195683) (1 like, 2 replies, 26 views).
3. **People want evaluation beyond pass/fail scores.** Requests include tracking human interventions, judging communication with people, and testing visual assistance in closed-loop tasks. [@Aish_krrr](https://x.com/Aish_krrr) [asked for an intervention leaderboard](https://x.com/Aish_krrr/status/2123924787059912794) (4 likes, 4 replies, 137 views); [@OutsourcedLogic](https://x.com/OutsourcedLogic) [requested public-rated communication benchmarks](https://x.com/OutsourcedLogic/status/2123939297208447376) (1 reply, 19 views).
4. **Infrastructure claims need careful attribution.** Posts connect AI progress to compute and local power concerns, but the data-centre thread also challenges the source of its energy and water estimates. [@4xCaliban](https://x.com/4xCaliban) [focused on compute and capital](https://x.com/4xCaliban/status/2123927756836667765) (102 likes, 8 replies, 2,533 views); [@JamesMelville](https://x.com/JamesMelville) [raised local resource concerns](https://x.com/JamesMelville/status/2123935097686573222) (79 likes, 6 replies, 2,172 views).
