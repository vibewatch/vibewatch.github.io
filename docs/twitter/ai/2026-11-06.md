# Twitter AI - 2026-11-06

## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Evaluation is shifting toward task completion and repeatability (→)

Four benchmark posts measured things static model rankings can miss: repeated scientific reasoning, mid-generation video edits, product setup, and performance on work-like tasks. [@ArtificialAnlys](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys) [reported](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2118752670030700953) (97 likes, 21 replies, 11,161 views) that VIALS-AA tests 161 visual life-science tasks across eight domains and counts a task only when the model succeeds on all three runs. Its results also expose cost differences: Sol scored 59% at about $0.056 per task, while the top-scoring Gemini 4 Carbon reached 64% at roughly 23 times that cost.

![VIALS-AA model scores and cost comparisons](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWdSUyKWoAEMJ-M.jpg)

[@alifcoder](https://x.com/alifcoder) [outlined](https://x.com/alifcoder/status/2118680759758442694) (14 likes, 13 replies, 1,023 views) StreamEval's tests for whether streaming video models follow changing instructions, preserve state, and edit generated content. The post says 38 models were evaluated; the [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28402) reports results from 37. Its 300 scripted cases and ten metrics cover five task types.

![StreamEval leaderboard and task coverage](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWcQ66Ab0AA6jZv.png)

![StreamEval benchmark task examples](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWcQ7GIbMAAGkyn.png)

[@Tabbu_ai](https://x.com/Tabbu_ai) [highlighted](https://x.com/Tabbu_ai/status/2118801890280689947) (35 likes, 8 replies, 2,438 views) Scope's database benchmark: only 16.6% of 1,035 agent sessions reached a first working result across 18 hosted products. The [leaderboard](https://www.tryscope.com/leaderboard/databases) records CAPTCHAs, bot blocks, rejected email domains, and OAuth-only sign-in among the barriers; 50 successful sessions used a working database before account creation. @Tabbu_ai explicitly asked for more evaluations of agents on real products.

[@DanielMiessler](https://x.com/DanielMiessler) [said](https://x.com/DanielMiessler/status/2118820785888633321) (2 replies, 770 views) Artificial Analysis benchmarks do not match his real-world experience, and described building Explain, Science, and Ship benchmarks. The [live dashboard](https://thebenchmarks.ai) presents model scores alongside cost; this is his own comparison, not an independent validation.

![Daniel Miessler's benchmark dashboard comparing scores and cost](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWePng6agAAhmBU.jpg)

### 1.2 Model and acceleration claims come with evidence limits (→)

[@eliebakouch](https://x.com/eliebakouch) [praised](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2118512391331729860) (108 likes, 13 replies, 4,738 views) Inkling 2 Small, citing a 17B-active, 594B-total model trained on 12T tokens. The [release page](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-small/) confirms the parameter counts and says the model launched on Tinker with closed weights; an Apache 2.0 weight release was planned after external safety testing. Its attached graphic details training and benchmark results, which remain company-reported.

![Inkling 2 Small training and benchmark summary](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWZ3V6NXQAAmSOV.jpg)

[@YSenqiao](https://x.com/YSenqiao) [announced](https://x.com/YSenqiao/status/2118503242472558751) (4 likes, 1 reply, 134 views) Microsoft's open Mage-VL-Cowork, a 4B computer-use model. Its [technical report](https://microsoft.github.io/Mage/cowork/assets/Mage-VL-Cowork.pdf) reports 66.0% on OSWorld-Verified under a 50-step budget; the chart compares model size and score and marks human performance at 72.4%.

![Mage-VL-Cowork's 4B OSWorld-Verified score compared with larger models and human performance](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWZuqGmXAAAerAj.jpg)

METR's [assessment](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/) estimates 1.9x faster growth in Anthropic's AECI benchmark scores since October 2025, with a 95% confidence interval of 1.5–2.8. METR says much of its most informative evidence was redacted and that the link between benchmark scores and broader real-world capability is uncertain. [@fleetingbits](https://x.com/fleetingbits) [summarized](https://x.com/fleetingbits/status/2118485551678648351) (26 likes, 4 replies, 3,152 views) that adding earlier models from other providers changes the estimate to 1.4x, and argued for more standardized reporting. That 1.4x comparison is the author's analysis, not METR's headline estimate.

[@ChinmayKak](https://x.com/ChinmayKak) [offered](https://x.com/ChinmayKak/status/2118795521196458187) (11 likes, 554 views) a separate interpretation that benchmark suites may also be changing over time. The post does not establish that claim, but its chart was part of the day's discussion about how to read acceleration estimates.

![Chart shared in discussion of the METR acceleration estimate](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWd47ASa0AA3xO2.jpg)

## 2. What Frustrates People

### Agent onboarding breaks before the first useful result

In Scope's tested setup, only 16.6% of 1,035 sessions reached a working database; 373 sessions stopped at CAPTCHAs and 105 at bot blocks. Seven of the 18 products had no successful sessions. Letting agents use a working database before account creation helped in 50 successful runs. This is a concrete blocker in the benchmark, not an estimate of all developer products. **Severity: High within the tested task. Build signal: Strong for agent-compatible onboarding, with broader prevalence still unmeasured.** [@Tabbu_ai](https://x.com/Tabbu_ai) [summarized](https://x.com/Tabbu_ai/status/2118801890280689947) (35 likes, 8 replies, 2,438 views) the [results and leaderboard](https://www.tryscope.com/leaderboard/databases).

### Trust and coordination remain visible failure points

[@swangentr](https://x.com/swangentr) [promoted](https://x.com/swangentr/status/2118739597240775129) (96 likes, 8 replies, 19,722 views) a financial assistant. In its replies, [@ProjectAglaya](https://x.com/ProjectAglaya) [said](https://x.com/ProjectAglaya/status/2118742522424082711) (47 views) users need a veto before an irreversible action, and [@kylekane](https://x.com/kylekane) [asked](https://x.com/kylekane/status/2118743823472017834) (57 views) what checks prevent bad advice. [@TammyBuilds](https://x.com/TammyBuilds) [reported](https://x.com/TammyBuilds/status/2118748752005538278) (9 likes, 17 replies, 300 views) token waste and mutual hallucination after putting 16 agents in one chat. These are isolated reports, so their frequency is unknown. **Severity: Medium in the described workflows. Build signal: Exploratory for approval gates, bounded handoffs, and independent review.**

### Benchmark results do not always match a user's work

[@DanielMiessler](https://x.com/DanielMiessler) [said](https://x.com/DanielMiessler/status/2118820785888633321) (2 replies, 770 views) Artificial Analysis benchmarks do not match his experience and built three alternatives. Separately, METR says its AECI trend estimate depends on analysis choices and may not transfer to broader tasks. That makes leaderboard-to-workflow fit a real limitation, but the discussion does not establish how common the mismatch is. **Severity: Medium for choosing systems from scores alone. Build signal: Moderate for evaluations tied to users' actual tasks and explicit uncertainty.**

## 6. New and Notable

### Agent-guided inference optimization

The [Together Compiler paper](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.together-compiler-continual-ai-inference) describes a persistent engine that lets agents test candidate inference changes without restarting the full runtime. It reports up to 2.6x higher throughput than tested open-source engines on selected frontier-model workloads and says the system serves Together AI's production fleet. [@askalphaxiv](https://x.com/askalphaxiv) [summarized](https://x.com/askalphaxiv/status/2118637014882963553) (16 likes, 2 replies, 1,027 views) the release; the throughput result is workload-specific, not a general guarantee.

### R&D acceleration estimate with substantial uncertainty

METR's new [assessment](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/) estimates a 1.9x increase in Anthropic's AECI score-growth rate, with a 95% confidence interval of 1.5–2.8. It notes that most of its most informative evidence was redacted and flags uncertainty about generalizing beyond well-specified tasks. [@fleetingbits](https://x.com/fleetingbits) [highlighted](https://x.com/fleetingbits/status/2118485551678648351) (26 likes, 4 replies, 3,152 views) the reporting limitations and called for more standardized disclosure.

## 8. Takeaways

1. **Agents still fail before doing useful work.** Scope measured a first-working-result rate of 16.6% across 1,035 database setup sessions, with signup and bot defenses among the blockers. [@Tabbu_ai](https://x.com/Tabbu_ai) [summarized](https://x.com/Tabbu_ai/status/2118801890280689947) (35 likes, 8 replies, 2,438 views) the benchmark.
