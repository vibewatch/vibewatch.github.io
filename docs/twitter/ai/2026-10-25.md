## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Public benchmark reliability is questioned 🡕

[@HamelHusain](https://x.com/HamelHusain) [criticized](https://x.com/HamelHusain/status/2114434195854684330) (219 likes, 44 replies, 8,610 views) the quality of public benchmarks and recommended inspecting grader failures before trusting a score. The attached Epoch summary labels several audits flawed, including documented scoring defects in Terminal-Bench, DeepSWE, OSWorld, and HealthBench Professional. Epoch’s benchmark hub tracks 94 benchmarks.

![Epoch summary table of audited benchmark scoring defects and false-negative issues](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HVf5Q0ZbwAAz2Xl.jpg)

**Discussion insight:** A reply from [@ivanleomk](https://x.com/ivanleomk) [said](https://x.com/ivanleomk/status/2114440719805071556) (12 likes, 4 replies, 473 views) their team had found similar problems auditing benchmarks; the reply mentions a wide confidence interval but does not name the benchmark.

### 1.2 Open models target agentic coding and cowork 🡕

[@nanbeige](https://x.com/nanbeige) [announced](https://x.com/nanbeige/status/2114328933181333798) (568 likes, 51 replies, 34,295 views) Nanbeige4.3-33B as a teacher model for smaller agent models. Its [Hugging Face page](https://huggingface.co/Nanbeige/Nanbeige4.3-33B) publishes comparisons on coding, cowork, and other tasks using the same stated inference settings and harness across models. The table shows strong results on several tasks but not a lead on every benchmark.

**Comparison to prior day:** Yesterday’s feedback focused on one user’s experience with model intent-following; today’s benchmark discussion raises a broader measurement concern about the graders and tasks behind model rankings. [@tenobrus](https://x.com/tenobrus) [reported](https://x.com/tenobrus/status/2113795375220093016) (202 likes, 23 replies, 5,793 views) a task-fit issue on October 24.
