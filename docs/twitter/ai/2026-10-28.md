## 2. What Frustrates People

### Context migration and workflow complexity

The SIE announcement notes that changing embedding models still requires re-embedding an index, even when API-compatible clients avoid a rewrite. In a separate interview summary, one author argues that elaborate agent workflows often fail to justify their complexity and recommends design documents, staged changes, and correctness checks. These are specific engineering trade-offs, not broad prevalence estimates.
