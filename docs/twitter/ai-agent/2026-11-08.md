## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 The harness is becoming the product (🡕)

Several distinct posts focus on the systems around the model: tools, context, memory, safety, orchestration, and verification. A source-code study of 11 coding-agent systems grouped them into seven recurring harness subsystems; it reports skills in 9 of 11 and MCP in 8 of 11, and says none of the studied runtimes imported LangChain, LangGraph, or AutoGen. [@slash1sol](https://x.com/slash1sol) summarized the study ([post](https://x.com/slash1sol/status/2119397444349784100)) (91 likes, 4 reposts, 36 replies, 1,964 views, 60 bookmarks).

![Abstract of a source-code study describing the anatomy of eleven coding-agent systems](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWmcvPrXYAEWnZc.png)

Research on Evolver tests a complementary approach: store useful procedural experience from earlier runs and retrieve or revise it for later tasks, without changing model parameters. The paper reports success rising from 83.13% to 87.50% across a selected set of 320 paired runs, with recorded token use down 24.24%; these are the authors’ evaluation results, not a general guarantee. [@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0) highlighted the results ([post](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2119473781689421855)) (108 likes, 9 reposts, 64 replies, 6 quotes, 7,832 views, 88 bookmarks). ## 2. What Frustrates People

### Agent reliability and recovery

**Medium confidence, limited evidence.** The Hermes reviewer reports delayed steering, brittle computer use, configuration changes going wrong with weaker models, and usage limits; Ash Hart says the chief of staff fixes intern work 25–30% of the time ([Hermes review](https://x.com/Da7_Tech/status/2119460293398937811)) (175 likes, 17 reposts, 32 replies, 22 quotes, 8,251 views, 118 bookmarks); ([Ash Hart reply](https://x.com/ashxhart/status/2119240489639804969)) (2 likes, 29 views). These are individual reports, not evidence of how common the failures are. Checkpoints, review, and explicit approval are the observed coping approaches. The posts make reliability worth addressing, but do not quantify willingness to pay.

### Verification can miss the actual requirement

**Medium confidence, one detailed critique.** A separate evaluator may validate that a feature works as specified while both builder and evaluator share an incomplete specification. The post’s export example is explicitly fictional; its concrete recommendation is to add acceptance cases from customer examples and underlying records, rather than treating two agent approvals as independent evidence ([post](https://x.com/krishnan/status/2119513725598306472)) (2 likes, 4 replies, 137 views, 2 bookmarks). This is a plausible engineering gap raised in one post, not a measured frequency.

## 3. What People Wish Existed

### Approval and recovery built into everyday agent use

**Practical need; direct opportunity.** One workflow author explicitly recommends approval before any send, payment, post, or merge; another user asks for safer configuration handling. A Hermes plugin’s approval-gated rollback and filesystem checkpoints partially address this need ([workflow post](https://x.com/cyrilXBT/status/2119324053496606959)) (66 likes, 8 reposts, 7 replies, 1 quote, 4,268 views, 39 bookmarks); ([Hermes review](https://x.com/Da7_Tech/status/2119460293398937811)) (175 likes, 17 reposts, 32 replies, 22 quotes, 8,251 views, 118 bookmarks); [plugin listing](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/marketplace/hermes-chronos). The urgency is explicit in the approval recommendations, but not measured across users.

### Acceptance tests independent of the builder

**Practical need; direct opportunity.** The proposed gap is not another agent approval, but test cases grounded in customer examples, edge conditions, and underlying records. Anthropic’s harness article describes a generator/evaluator workflow; the critique argues that independent acceptance evidence is still needed when both agents negotiate the same incomplete bar ([post](https://x.com/krishnan/status/2119513725598306472)) (2 likes, 4 replies, 137 views, 2 bookmarks); [Anthropic article](https://www.anthropic.com/research/harness-design-long-running-apps).

### Reusable experience with safeguards against bad lessons

**Practical need; competitive opportunity.** Evolver and ACE both describe ways to reuse experience without retraining the model, so this is already an active research area rather than an unaddressed idea. A reply asks how the system avoids preserving a bad lesson ([reply](https://x.com/Noxen_of/status/2119403006638928124)) (9 views); this is an open concern, not proof of a failure. [ACE post](https://x.com/Atenov_D/status/2119396318057570546) (28 likes, 16 replies, 666 views, 20 bookmarks); [Evolver paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07595); [ACE repository](https://github.com/ace-agent/ace).

## 7. Where the Opportunities Are

**[+++] Guarded execution and recovery** — Human approval appears in the personal-agent rebuild proposal; paper-only operation and no keys constrain the trading example; the Hermes plugin adds checkpoints and rollback. The need recurs across distinct workflow and product posts, although no adoption or willingness-to-pay data is reported ([workflow post](https://x.com/cyrilXBT/status/2119324053496606959)) (66 likes, 8 reposts, 7 replies, 1 quote, 4,268 views, 39 bookmarks); ([Crypto Claude Desk post](https://x.com/RoundtableSpace/status/2119524105766502884)) (41 likes, 14 replies, 2 quotes, 53,219 views, 44 bookmarks); ([Chronos post](https://x.com/MossAIStudio/status/2119332706568097944)) (23 likes, 4 replies, 2 quotes, 4,299 views, 7 bookmarks).

**[++] Reusable context with provenance and correction** — Evolver and ACE report improvements from retaining task experience; a reply to the ACE post asks how a bad lesson is filtered ([reply](https://x.com/Noxen_of/status/2119403006638928124)) (9 views). A reliable memory loop that records its evidence and supports correction is a research-backed, but still unproven-at-scale, opportunity ([Evolver paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07595); [ACE repository](https://github.com/ace-agent/ace)).

**[++] Independent acceptance evidence** — The Anthropic harness article and one detailed critique point to a gap between an implementation passing negotiated tests and satisfying the original need. Tests grounded in customer examples and underlying records are a concrete opportunity, but this date provides one critique rather than a prevalence estimate ([post](https://x.com/krishnan/status/2119513725598306472)) (2 likes, 4 replies, 137 views, 2 bookmarks); [Anthropic article](https://www.anthropic.com/research/harness-design-long-running-apps).

## 8. Takeaways

1. **The harness is a major focus.** Posts cover system anatomy, persistent experience, tool use, safety, and verification; the Evolver paper reports a bounded evaluation result rather than a universal gain ([study post](https://x.com/slash1sol/status/2119397444349784100)) (91 likes, 4 reposts, 36 replies, 1,964 views, 60 bookmarks); ([Evolver post](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2119473781689421855)) (108 likes, 9 reposts, 64 replies, 6 quotes, 7,832 views, 88 bookmarks).
2. **Agent count is not the same as a well-run system.** The author describing 98 bots says a rebuild would use one chief of staff, explicit gates, and fewer routines ([post](https://x.com/cyrilXBT/status/2119324053496606959)) (66 likes, 8 reposts, 7 replies, 1 quote, 4,268 views, 39 bookmarks).
3. **Human control and recovery are concrete design choices.** The evidence includes approval before external actions, paper-only trading, commit-bound tests, and filesystem rollback; these are product and workflow examples, not proof of broad adoption ([TensorFold workflow](https://x.com/ashxhart/status/2119201973438185659)) (129 likes, 6 reposts, 41 replies, 5 quotes, 4,486 views, 123 bookmarks); ([Chronos post](https://x.com/MossAIStudio/status/2119332706568097944)) (23 likes, 4 replies, 2 quotes, 4,299 views, 7 bookmarks).
4. **Passing a test is not enough if the acceptance bar is incomplete.** One detailed critique recommends evidence independent of the builder, while explicitly labeling its export scenario fictional ([post](https://x.com/krishnan/status/2119513725598306472)) (2 likes, 4 replies, 137 views, 2 bookmarks).
