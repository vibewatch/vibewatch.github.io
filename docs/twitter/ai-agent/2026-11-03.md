# Twitter AI Agent - 2026-11-03

## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Agent harnesses become the engineering surface (↑)

Research and practitioner posts focused on the layer around the model: tools, context, permissions, orchestration, and verification. The evidence spans repository analysis, benchmark results, a compiler-correctness study, and one detailed operating workflow.

[@N01ennn](https://x.com/N01ennn) [summarized](https://x.com/N01ennn/status/2117584818070249641) (57 likes, 3 retweets, 23 replies, 2,415 views, 60 bookmarks) a study of 11 coding-agent systems that groups their harnesses into seven recurring subsystems. Its authors report that skills appear in 9 of 11 systems and MCP in 8 of 11. The [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00006) also describes a small starter harness and design recommendations.

![Diagram summarizing seven coding-agent harness subsystems, including tools, context, permissions, orchestration, and extensibility](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWMqk0sWIAAiBvs.png)

[@beamnxw](https://x.com/beamnxw) [described](https://x.com/beamnxw/status/2117644466504786016) (77 likes, 4 retweets, 35 replies, 2,377 views, 78 bookmarks) Harmony Harness, a research system that uses feedback from agent runs to evolve tools, skills, and workflows. The [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15150) reports that 66.7% of 30 out-of-distribution tasks passed both functionality and security checks, versus 36.7% for an unevolved harness; this is a paper result, not an independent replication.

![Harmony Harness research image showing an agent workflow that evaluates and revises harness components](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWNiakZWoAA805X.jpg)

[@PatrickToulme](https://x.com/PatrickToulme) [reported](https://x.com/PatrickToulme/status/2117721535767253071) (71 likes, 4 retweets, 6 replies, 3,037 views, 46 bookmarks) that specialized harnesses and agent swarms helped find and fix XLA compiler bugs. The coauthored [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00624) reports 142 resolved bugs and 103 findings under review. In discussion, a reader asked how duplicate discoveries were handled; Toulme replied that a lead agent manages deduplication.

[@gregisenberg](https://x.com/gregisenberg) [outlined](https://x.com/gregisenberg/status/2117734977312047537) (158 likes, 6 retweets, 68 replies, 12,770 views, 296 bookmarks) a company workflow spanning research, specs, design alternatives, parallel coding, browser tests, bug fixes, and funnel review. The author says agents handle routine work while people provide creative feedback and correct mistakes; when asked whether humans remain necessary, he answered yes. This is one operator's account, not a measured productivity study.

**Comparison to prior day:** Nov. 2 centered on persistent project memory, shared skills, and adapting to changing tools. Today's posts make the harness itself the object of testing and improvement, with explicit functionality and security outcomes.

### 1.2 Skills spread, but selection and maintenance become problems (↑)

Skills appeared both as a platform feature and as a reliability problem in larger libraries. [@viticci](https://x.com/viticci) [pointed to](https://x.com/viticci/status/2117722301374578773) (120 likes, 8 retweets, 5 replies, 7,527 views, 39 bookmarks) Apple's [App Intents documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/orchestrating-complex-actions-with-app-skills), which describes app skills and Markdown guidance for `SKILL.md` files. A reply asked whether system search can index these files, reflecting a practical discoverability question.

![Apple App Intents documentation image illustrating app skills and their Markdown guidance](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWOpNPbWkAAP814.jpg)

[@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai) [summarized](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2117612013258228030) (39 likes, 9 retweets, 7 replies, 2,491 views, 39 bookmarks) the [Skill++ preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00544), which addresses similar skill names crowding out the right procedure. Across a 731-skill evaluation, the paper reports required-skill selection of 76.3% with revised descriptions versus 36.4% with original metadata, across three benchmarks and five routers.

![Skill++ research image showing results for selecting required skills from a growing library](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWNE5yMa8AAoxWW.jpg)

**Comparison to prior day:** Nov. 2's discussion emphasized sharing and updating skills across agents. Today adds a platform-level example from Apple and a specific failure mode: a skill can remain available yet be selected incorrectly when its description overlaps another.

### 1.3 Security and control move into the product layer (↑)

Security was framed both as a built-in coding workflow and as an operational risk. [@rauchg](https://x.com/rauchg) [announced](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2117734833824948373) (143 likes, 3 retweets, 28 replies, 11,250 views, 81 bookmarks) that Vercel's [Security Review](https://x.com/vercel/status/2117726156992753721) entered public beta, scanning pushes and offering fixes through Vercel Agent. A reply raised the risk of noisy alerts; the CEO said the signal-to-noise ratio was high but did not provide a measurement.

[@grinich](https://x.com/grinich) [described](https://x.com/grinich/status/2117400083151138954) (167 likes, 9 retweets, 22 replies, 28,522 views, 74 bookmarks) an October 30 WorkOS incident involving internal go-to-market systems, while saying no customer platform data was accessed. The CEO attributed the activity to ARTEX; replies questioned the technical basis for that attribution, and the linked statement said the investigation was continuing.

**Comparison to prior day:** Nov. 2 raised trust and recourse questions around agents handling transactions. Today's discussion adds a security-review product and a reported incident, while leaving attribution and alert-quality claims appropriately unsettled.
