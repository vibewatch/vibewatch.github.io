# Twitter AI Agent - 2026-10-24

## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Moving from agent trials to organizational systems 🡕

Two consulting perspectives describe work needed between tool adoption and production-scale agent operations. [@mardehaym](https://x.com/mardehaym) [outlined](https://x.com/mardehaym/status/2113990724282724742) (149 likes, 50 replies, 22,111 views, 251 bookmarks) a seven-step progression covering department context, harnesses, headless execution, governance, and lifecycle management. [@vasuman](https://x.com/vasuman) [argued](https://x.com/vasuman/status/2114092422112432553) (160 likes, 28 replies, 8,598 views, 182 bookmarks) that workflow redesign, data access, human review, and internal systems matter more than workshops alone. Both posts are practitioner or company perspectives, not population-wide adoption studies.

### 1.2 Agent runtimes are accumulating first-party integrations 🡕

Nous Research's Hermes roundup lists 18 plugins for delegation, imports, MCP, memory, tracing and cost, security scanning, sandboxing, and external applications. [@HermesWatcher](https://x.com/HermesWatcher) [shared](https://x.com/HermesWatcher/status/2113797636918739018) (90 likes, 6 replies, 3,884 views, 107 bookmarks) the list and a diagram of the plugin categories.

## 2. What Frustrates People

### Organizations stall between tool adoption and governed operations

The consulting framework describes companies remaining at tool access while lacking shared context, headless execution, governance, and lifecycle management. A separate company-positioning post says data access, internal incentives, and workflow redesign are harder than offering skills or workshops. These are practitioner perspectives; this date does not quantify how broadly the barriers occur.

---

## 3. What People Wish Existed

### A managed path from experiments to governed workflows

The seven-step framework calls for context, harnesses, headless execution, access controls, and lifecycle management rather than isolated tools. [@mardehaym](https://x.com/mardehaym) [describes](https://x.com/mardehaym/status/2113990724282724742) (149 likes, 50 replies, 22,111 views, 251 bookmarks) a progression toward governed, scheduled operations. **Opportunity: competitive** with consulting and platform offerings; the post identifies a process gap but does not compare solutions.
