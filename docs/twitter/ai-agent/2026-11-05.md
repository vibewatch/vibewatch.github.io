# Twitter AI Agent - 2026-11-05

## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Evaluation shifts from agent count to outcomes and cost (🡒)

Evaluation posts emphasize task results, cost, and reproducible checks rather than the number of agents in a workflow. The evidence spans a coding-harness benchmark, a scientific-agent grading workflow, and a voice-agent benchmark announcement; the benchmark and product claims remain attributable to their authors.

[@HelloSurgeAI](https://x.com/HelloSurgeAI) [reported](https://x.com/HelloSurgeAI/status/2118109425697173658) (14 likes, 1 retweet, 9 replies, 1,022 views, 8 bookmarks) a first-party test of four coding setups on 100 real software tasks. Its chart shows a 31.1/100 vanilla baseline; Superpowers gained 2.4 points at 1.1x cost, `/goal` plus `/code-review` gained 3.3 at 1.8x, and Compound Engineering gained 1.8 at 8x cost. Higher thinking effort gained 11.3 at 3.5x cost. The [full analysis](https://surgehq.ai/blog/benchmarking-coding-agent-harnesses) says the 8x setup had no statistically clear improvement; the post also describes a 17-agent run that missed a critical issue. These are Surge's own results, not independent validation.

![Surge AI's benchmark chart comparing score gains and relative costs across coding-agent harnesses and thinking effort](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWUJLBvXMAADML_.jpg)

[@aitonomyfound](https://x.com/aitonomyfound) [described](https://x.com/aitonomyfound/status/2118372269051236736) (22 likes, 3 retweets, 2 replies, 3,210 views, 4 bookmarks) a scientific-agent evaluation workflow: pinned Claude Code or Codex profiles, isolated tasks, frozen outputs, separate programmatic verifiers, held-out data, and retained traces. Its [Science's Last Exam documentation](https://sciences-last-exam.org/docs) describes the benchmark and says public downloads were scheduled for Nov. 6. This is the project's account of its own methodology.

![Science's Last Exam evaluation workflow, from environment preparation and task staging through agent runs, independent grading, and retained evidence](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWX3LJ6boAAeUtI.png)

[@maddieworks](https://x.com/maddieworks) [quoted](https://x.com/maddieworks/status/2118394337092349954) (33 likes, 15 replies, 2,577 views, 4 bookmarks) a Cekura Bench launch that claims coverage of 50-plus voice models and platforms. Its [public benchmark site](https://benchmarks.cekura.ai/) groups comparisons across speech-to-speech, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and voice-agent platforms under documented conditions. Those coverage claims come from the benchmark publisher.

**Comparison to prior day:** The previous day's report already included Oumi's proposal to turn recurring harness failures into evaluations and training data [announcement](https://x.com/oumi_ai/status/2117992968347181298) (35 likes, 5 retweets, 3 replies, 3,254 views, 7 bookmarks). Today's posts add a quantified coding-harness comparison and a concrete verifier workflow; they do not establish a rise in adoption.

### 1.3 Harness design includes memory, dependencies, and recovery (🡒)

Two diagrams frame the agent as one component in a wider execution system: one lays out operational harness concerns, and a new survey classifies how graphs connect reasoning, memory, tools, and coordination.

[@ConsciousRide](https://x.com/ConsciousRide) [outlined](https://x.com/ConsciousRide/status/2118420880804196522) (16 likes, 3 retweets, 8 replies, 442 views, 15 bookmarks) ten areas around an agent loop: context and memory, tool contracts, permissions and state, sandboxes, retries, idempotency, completion checks, traces, evaluations, and recovery. The author argues that a harness makes errors visible and recoverable; this is a practitioner framework, not a measured result.

![Harness diagram showing context, tools, permissions, retries, verification, traces, evaluations, recovery, and sandboxing around an agent execution loop](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWYkieSaAAA66p_.png)

[@TheYotg](https://x.com/TheYotg) [summarized](https://x.com/TheYotg/status/2118303352953979338) (6 likes, 2 replies, 333 views, 8 bookmarks) a new [graph-mediated LLM systems survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05953v1). It gives an example where retrieval returns the updated operator but a stored notification plan still targets the previous one, so a correct lookup can still lead to a wrong action. The survey distinguishes graph roles from where they are computed and recommends evaluating downstream effects, process diagnostics, and full system costs.

![Graph-mediated agent systems taxonomy distinguishing reasoning, evidence, retrieval, execution, and coordination roles from computation loci](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWW5rAoWUAAgDFO.png)

## 2. What Frustrates People

### Harness cost can outpace measurable gains

**Severity: Medium; prevalence: unmeasured.** In its 100-task test, [@HelloSurgeAI](https://x.com/HelloSurgeAI) [reported](https://x.com/HelloSurgeAI/status/2118109425697173658) (14 likes, 1 retweet, 9 replies, 1,022 views, 8 bookmarks) that Compound Engineering cost 8x the vanilla setup without a statistically clear improvement. The same test found a larger score gain from higher thinking effort, but at 3.5x cost. This supports cost-aware evaluation for teams considering complex agent stacks, not a general conclusion that harnesses do not help.

### Voice agents can fabricate missing customer details

**Severity: Medium in this example; prevalence: one report.** [@5uck1ess](https://x.com/5uck1ess) [described](https://x.com/5uck1ess/status/2118394683596345564) (2 likes, 2 replies, 15 views) a Grok Bot phone agent that handled a pricing call, then invented an email address when asked to send pricing and saved it for the team. The post does not establish how often this happens. Explicit confirmation before recording customer-provided details is a plausible need, but this single account is not a prevalence measure.

### Cloud shutdowns put workspace portability in focus

**Severity: Medium if work is cloud-only; prevalence: unmeasured.** [@HaStrive](https://x.com/HaStrive) [summarized](https://x.com/HaStrive/status/2118126221343268971) (1 like, 2 replies, 134 views, 1 bookmark) a Conductor Cloud notice as read-only on Nov. 13, with shutdown and data deletion beginning Nov. 20, and urged users to transfer workspaces or export data. This is one account's summary, not evidence that users have already lost work or that the experience is widespread.

![A user's Conductor Cloud timeline summary showing the reported read-only date, shutdown, data-deletion date, and export or transfer option](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HWUYkRQbsAAMQx5.jpg)

## 3. What People Wish Existed

The clearest direct request is finer-grained cost visibility. A second, narrower need is portable agent workspaces; neither signal establishes broad demand.

### Cost attribution down to individual tool calls

[@atomicbyte_](https://x.com/atomicbyte_) [asked](https://x.com/atomicbyte_/status/2118438205666255323) (1 like, 2 replies, 19 views) whether Sentry's agent tracing can show which tool call drove a cost spike or only totals per conversation. Sentry's [post](https://x.com/sentry/status/2118427253084463353) (14 likes, 3 retweets, 8 replies, 758 views, 2 bookmarks) says its tools track LLM costs and alert on spend spikes, but the reply asks for a more specific breakdown. **Need type:** Practical. **Urgency:** Not stated. **Opportunity: Direct**, based on one explicit question rather than demonstrated broad demand.

The cost question also appears in [@iiiichigo_chan's repository-analysis summary](https://x.com/iiiichigo_chan/status/2118397992239571196) (61 likes, 21 replies, 3,594 views, 63 bookmarks): the author says only 10 of 217 tracked projects reported cost, a reading the author [confirmed in a reply](https://x.com/iiiichigo_chan/status/2118403239045656906) (27 views). Treat that figure as the account's summary, not an independent estimate.

### Workspaces that remain transferable

[@HaStrive](https://x.com/HaStrive) [urged](https://x.com/HaStrive/status/2118126221343268971) (1 like, 2 replies, 134 views, 1 bookmark) Conductor Cloud users to transfer workspaces or export data before the dates in the account's service-notice summary. **Need type:** Practical and time-bound for affected users. **Opportunity: Competitive**, since [@stretchcloud](https://x.com/stretchcloud) [describes](https://x.com/stretchcloud/status/2118315894858207710) (1 like, 5 replies, 128 views) Campfire as a self-hosted multi-agent workspace. The evidence supports a portability concern and one builder response, not a market-wide request.

## 7. Where the Opportunities Are

**[+++] Task-level evaluation with cost and trace attribution** - Surge's 100-task benchmark shows a large cost spread between harness setups, while its 8x-cost configuration had no statistically clear improvement [benchmark post](https://x.com/HelloSurgeAI/status/2118109425697173658) (14 likes, 1 retweet, 9 replies, 1,022 views, 8 bookmarks). Science's Last Exam describes separate grading and retained traces [method post](https://x.com/aitonomyfound/status/2118372269051236736) (22 likes, 3 retweets, 2 replies, 3,210 views, 4 bookmarks), and a Sentry reply asks for cost visibility at the tool-call level [question](https://x.com/atomicbyte_/status/2118438205666255323) (1 like, 2 replies, 19 views). A product that joins per-task success, tool-level spend, and replayable evidence would address a specific gap; the evidence supports the opportunity, not a market-size estimate.

**[++] Portable workspaces and user-owned agent state** - One account's Conductor Cloud summary urges users to transfer or export before a reported shutdown [post](https://x.com/HaStrive/status/2118126221343268971) (1 like, 2 replies, 134 views, 1 bookmark), and Campfire's builder describes a self-hosted alternative [post](https://x.com/stretchcloud/status/2118315894858207710) (1 like, 5 replies, 128 views). OpenClaw and Perplexity also pitch shared context across people and tasks. Portability is a credible design requirement, but the service notice is one account's summary and no migration is confirmed.

**[+] Voice-agent confirmation and interruption checks** - One operator says a phone agent invented and saved an email address rather than asking for one [post](https://x.com/5uck1ess/status/2118394683596345564) (2 likes, 2 replies, 15 views); in a separate voice-agent discussion, [@FenixTechX](https://x.com/FenixTechX) [said](https://x.com/FenixTechX/status/2118398702578487314) (7 views) agents still struggle with basic interruptions. These examples suggest test cases for explicit confirmation and turn-taking, but do not establish prevalence.

## 8. Takeaways

1. **More agents did not guarantee better results in the cited coding test.** Surge reports that higher thinking effort outscored its tested harness additions, while its 8x-cost setup showed no statistically clear improvement [benchmark post](https://x.com/HelloSurgeAI/status/2118109425697173658) (14 likes, 1 retweet, 9 replies, 1,022 views, 8 bookmarks).
2. **The evaluation conversation centers on verifiable outcomes and execution evidence.** Aitonomy describes separate graders and held-out data [method post](https://x.com/aitonomyfound/status/2118372269051236736) (22 likes, 3 retweets, 2 replies, 3,210 views, 4 bookmarks), while the graph survey argues for measuring downstream effects rather than only local graph metrics [survey summary](https://x.com/TheYotg/status/2118303352953979338) (6 likes, 2 replies, 333 views, 8 bookmarks).
3. **Shared context is becoming a product surface, but reliability and ownership remain open questions.** OpenClaw describes a shared agent and queue [launch](https://x.com/openclaw/status/2118404891911864536) (40 likes, 3 retweets, 6 replies, 5,275 views, 13 bookmarks), Perplexity announced web-based Teammates for Max subscribers [user-posted screenshots and quote](https://x.com/ChrisUniverse/status/2118412806554784195) (27 likes, 2 retweets, 7 replies, 2,079 views, 7 bookmarks), and Campfire's builder pitches self-hosting [post](https://x.com/stretchcloud/status/2118315894858207710) (1 like, 5 replies, 128 views).
4. **The concrete failure reports are important but sparse.** One operator says a voice agent invented a customer email [post](https://x.com/5uck1ess/status/2118394683596345564) (2 likes, 2 replies, 15 views); one Sentry reply asks for cost attribution by tool call [question](https://x.com/atomicbyte_/status/2118438205666255323) (1 like, 2 replies, 19 views). Neither establishes prevalence.
