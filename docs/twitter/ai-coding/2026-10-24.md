## 3. What People Wish Existed

### Stable model menus and clear change notices

The October 23 and 24 reports describe different access states in Antigravity, including models newly appearing for some trials and then missing from another user's selector. A clear per-account availability page and notices when model choices change would address the reported uncertainty. Opportunity: Direct.

### A low-effort way to turn demonstrated work into reusable skills

Skill Recorder's capture-and-review workflow addresses the burden of writing every procedure from scratch. Its source post also shows the tradeoff: generated steps are editable, so users can inspect what the agent will reuse. Opportunity: Competitive.
