## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Antigravity access varied across trial accounts (🡕)

Posts said some Jio Google AI Pro trial users could see Gemini 4 Argon and Anthropic models in Antigravity, while other users on the same trial could not. One author described the rollout as unconfirmed, so the evidence points to account-level inconsistency rather than a verified plan-wide change.

[@k2sbhai](https://x.com/k2sbhai) [reported](https://x.com/k2sbhai/status/2113563309635174878) (224 likes, 23 replies, 21,397 views, 140 bookmarks) that some trial subscribers had gained access after several days.
