### 1.3 Cross-session context is becoming a product feature 🡕

[@RoundtableSpace](https://x.com/RoundtableSpace) [described](https://x.com/RoundtableSpace/status/2115371744688996845) (33 likes, 18 replies, 34,889 views) agentlog, a CLI for searching session logs across Claude Code, Codex, and Copilot CLI.
