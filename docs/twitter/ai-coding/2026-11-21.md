# Twitter AI Coding - 2026-11-21

## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Harness reliability can matter as much as the model 🡒

Five posts raised connected issues around agents that stop early, skip tests, lose constraints, or advance after unfinished work. LangChain's published experiment offers a measurable example: with GPT-5.2-Codex held constant, its Deep Agents coding agent rose from 52.8 to 66.5 on Terminal-Bench 2.0 after changes to the harness, including self-verification and loop detection ([LangChain](https://www.langchain.com/blog/improving-deep-agents-with-harness-engineering)).

[@MaryamMiradi](https://x.com/MaryamMiradi) [outlined](https://x.com/MaryamMiradi/status/2123875734305345575) (18 likes, 20 replies, 626 views, 16 bookmarks) a five-layer harness covering agent loops, tools, context, memory, and evaluation. The attached map puts permissions and result checks inside a repeated context-model-tools-results cycle.

![Infographic mapping five agent-harness layers and the context, model, permissions, tools, results, and repeat loop](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HXmEKffXMAAYrVS.jpg)

[@vanshdot](https://x.com/vanshdot) [claimed](https://x.com/vanshdot/status/2123966532263055868) (2 likes, 2 replies, 20 views) that Gemini 4 Neon scored 12.8% in a minimal mini-SWE harness and 39.0% inside Antigravity, attributing the difference to the surrounding setup; the post did not link the evaluation. In separate practitioner accounts, [@johnmatsis](https://x.com/johnmatsis) [reported](https://x.com/johnmatsis/status/2123957429641572459) (1 like, 2 replies, 27 views) Codex hallucinations and unfinished milestones on a marketplace build, while [@jaxcore](https://x.com/jaxcore) [said](https://x.com/jaxcore/status/2123926707027505431) (3 replies, 37 views) schematic and CAD outputs needed several rounds and triple-checking.

## 2. What Frustrates People

### Agents can report progress without finishing or preserving constraints

**Severity: Medium to High.** Three practitioner posts described consequential but individual failures. [@johnmatsis](https://x.com/johnmatsis) [said](https://x.com/johnmatsis/status/2123957429641572459) (1 like, 2 replies, 27 views) Codex produced hallucinations on a marketplace build and advanced to later milestones while earlier ones were unfinished. [@jaxcore](https://x.com/jaxcore) [reported](https://x.com/jaxcore/status/2123926707027505431) (3 replies, 37 views) having to review schematic and CAD outputs over several rounds and triple-check them. [@arijitghosh02](https://x.com/arijitghosh02) [described](https://x.com/arijitghosh02/status/2123967158262579591) (2 replies, 12 views) an instruction disappearing after Claude Code compacted a long chat; the author now puts durable rules in `CLAUDE.md`. The observed sample is too small to estimate prevalence. Explicit completion checks, tests, and persistent project instructions are the workarounds surfaced today; LangChain's [experiment](https://www.langchain.com/blog/improving-deep-agents-with-harness-engineering) uses a pre-completion verification step and tests. This is a practical area to build for.

### Plan entitlements and usage costs are not always clear

**Severity: Medium.** [@ManningBlackall](https://x.com/ManningBlackall) [reported](https://x.com/ManningBlackall/status/2123954803655291106) (3 likes, 2 replies, 43 views) that a Workspace upgrade still left Neon unavailable in Gemini and Antigravity, with no clear next upgrade step. [@DevAdventur3s](https://x.com/DevAdventur3s) [said](https://x.com/DevAdventur3s/status/2123922615559434338) (2 replies, 197 views) an Astra API discount did not apply to Codex, the interface where the author used Astra. [@TekonBurns](https://x.com/TekonBurns) [described](https://x.com/TekonBurns/status/2123964615428276612) (6 likes, 3 replies, 422 views) a speed tier whose API price was scheduled to rise to six times standard pricing. The posts show specific eligibility and cost confusion, not a broad estimate of user dissatisfaction. A clearer plan-to-model matrix and usage-cost display would address the concrete friction reported.

### Local project history can cross account boundaries

**Severity: High potential impact, low observed prevalence.** [@SJFriedl](https://x.com/SJFriedl) [warned](https://x.com/SJFriedl/status/2123936740746707360) (1 like, 2 replies, 91 views) that Codex Desktop showed the same local projects and conversations after switching between Plus and Business accounts. The linked [Codex issue](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/35657) describes the same account-switching behavior, says clearing local data only removes it temporarily, and notes possible privacy implications. The direct coping action described in the issue is to clear local data, which the report says is not a viable account-isolation solution; durable per-account workspace separation is worth addressing.

## 3. What People Wish Existed

### Verifiable completion and durable task rules

**Practical need. Urgency: Medium to High. Opportunity: Direct.** The posts call for agents that prove a task is complete and retain important constraints after context compaction. In the harness thread, [@daraben_](https://x.com/daraben_) [recommended](https://x.com/daraben_/status/2123899473680330830) (1 like, 7 views) an explicit completion condition rather than letting the model decide when to stop. [@arijitghosh02](https://x.com/arijitghosh02) [recommended](https://x.com/arijitghosh02/status/2123967158262579591) (2 replies, 12 views) putting durable rules in `CLAUDE.md`. LangChain's [harness experiment](https://www.langchain.com/blog/improving-deep-agents-with-harness-engineering) partially addresses this with a pre-completion verification step and tests, while the thread also raises an unresolved design question: how to detect a loop without interrupting legitimate repeated edits.

### Account-isolated local workspaces

**Practical need. Urgency: High for teams handling confidential projects. Opportunity: Direct.** [@SJFriedl](https://x.com/SJFriedl) [asked for caution](https://x.com/SJFriedl/status/2123936740746707360) (1 like, 2 replies, 91 views) after Codex Desktop showed local projects and conversations across two accounts. The linked [public issue](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/35657) states that each account should have isolated local project and session state. The issue describes no lasting workaround, so account-aware isolation is the clearest unmet need in this set.

### Clear model availability and plan eligibility

**Practical need. Urgency: Medium. Opportunity: Direct.** [@ManningBlackall](https://x.com/ManningBlackall) [questioned](https://x.com/ManningBlackall/status/2123954803655291106) (3 likes, 2 replies, 43 views) why a paid Workspace upgrade did not make Neon available or show a clear upgrade route. [@DevAdventur3s](https://x.com/DevAdventur3s) [noted](https://x.com/DevAdventur3s/status/2123922615559434338) (2 replies, 197 views) that an Astra discount excluded Codex. The screenshots and posts show partial access through different plan types, but no single clear path to use a model in the user's preferred coding interface.

## 6. New and Notable

### OpenResearch Intelligence Index adds a model-plus-harness cost view

[@Ldataengineer](https://x.com/Ldataengineer) [summarized](https://x.com/Ldataengineer/status/2123942570069721590) (1 like, 2 replies, 154 views) alphaXiv's OpenResearch Intelligence Index, published Nov. 20. The result table compares eight model-harness pairs and reports mean cost per attempt: Opus 5.5 in Claude Code scored 0.618 at $16.46, while GPT-6.1 Sol in Codex scored 0.549 at $3.83. alphaXiv's [methodology](https://www.alphaxiv.org/blog/introducing-openresearch-intelligence-index) says the index covers science, post-training, and literature tasks, uses each provider's native coding harness, and is not directly comparable to source benchmark leaderboards; the project says its code is forthcoming. It is an autoresearch index, not a coding benchmark.

**[+++] Verifiable completion and persistent task context** — LangChain's [fixed-model harness experiment](https://www.langchain.com/blog/improving-deep-agents-with-harness-engineering) reports a move from 52.8 to 66.5 on Terminal-Bench 2.0, while [@MaryamMiradi](https://x.com/MaryamMiradi) [described](https://x.com/MaryamMiradi/status/2123875734305345575) (18 likes, 20 replies, 626 views, 16 bookmarks) the harness layers and practitioners [@johnmatsis](https://x.com/johnmatsis) [reported](https://x.com/johnmatsis/status/2123957429641572459) (1 like, 2 replies, 27 views) unfinished work and [@arijitghosh02](https://x.com/arijitghosh02) [described](https://x.com/arijitghosh02/status/2123967158262579591) (2 replies, 12 views) constraints lost to compaction. ## 8. Takeaways

1. **Harness design is part of model performance.** LangChain reports a 13.7-point gain with GPT-5.2-Codex held constant, and a separate post attributes a claimed Gemini 4 Neon score gap to Antigravity's harness ([LangChain](https://www.langchain.com/blog/improving-deep-agents-with-harness-engineering); [@vanshdot](https://x.com/vanshdot) [post](https://x.com/vanshdot/status/2123966532263055868) (2 likes, 2 replies, 20 views)).
2. **Completion still needs proof and durable context.** Users described unfinished milestones, manual verification, and instructions lost to compaction; [@daraben_](https://x.com/daraben_) [recommended](https://x.com/daraben_/status/2123899473680330830) (1 like, 7 views) an explicit exit check. [@johnmatsis](https://x.com/johnmatsis) [reported](https://x.com/johnmatsis/status/2123957429641572459) (1 like, 2 replies, 27 views) unfinished milestones, while [@arijitghosh02](https://x.com/arijitghosh02) [described](https://x.com/arijitghosh02/status/2123967158262579591) (2 replies, 12 views) constraints lost to compaction.
3. **Entitlements affect actual model choice.** A Workspace user said Neon remained unavailable after an upgrade, and an Astra API discount excluded Codex ([@ManningBlackall](https://x.com/ManningBlackall) [post](https://x.com/ManningBlackall/status/2123954803655291106) (3 likes, 2 replies, 43 views); [@DevAdventur3s](https://x.com/DevAdventur3s) [post](https://x.com/DevAdventur3s/status/2123922615559434338) (2 replies, 197 views)).
4. **Control and lower-cost integration are concrete build directions, not proven mass adoption.** omher routes bot work and approval, while venice-bridge connects a web subscription to local coding clients ([@rlaope](https://x.com/rlaope) [post](https://x.com/rlaope/status/2123968720733356248) (1 like, 2 replies, 26 views); [@ns0bj](https://x.com/ns0bj) [post](https://x.com/ns0bj/status/2123916196684640544) (2 likes, 3 replies, 70 views)).
