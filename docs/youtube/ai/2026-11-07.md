## 7. Where the Opportunities Are

These are opportunity hypotheses suggested by creator coverage, not validated buyer demand.

**[++] Current, transparent AI-video cost and rights comparisons** - Three videos compare free or low-cost generators and describe shifting credits, ads, watermarks, eligibility, and commercial-use terms. A regularly maintained comparison could help creators evaluate workflows; two videos come from the same sponsored creator, and the sample does not establish willingness to pay.

**[+] Source-linked AI capability and market claims** - CNN discusses uncertainty about frontier systems, while robotics and market videos repeat claims without primary test data or market sources. Clear labels separating company statements, creator interpretation, and independently measured results could improve traceability. No video explicitly asks for such a product.

**[+] Reusable no-code agent workflows with clear limits** - Dan Martell and AI Playbook show personal-task and creative workflows, but one names no agent platform and neither reports reliability or time savings. Templates that state required tools, permissions, and failure modes are an emerging idea, not demonstrated user demand.
