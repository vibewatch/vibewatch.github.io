### 1.2 Video-generation tutorials compete on access and shot control (↑)

Two videos demonstrate different ways to make AI-generated video more accessible or controllable. One compares services and free tiers; the other uses a 3D scene as a guide for generation.

[Malva AI](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCv3ZocWMnZw3aljHs4irOzA) demonstrates BytePlus/Seedance, an ad-supported option, and YouTube Create for text-to-video, image-to-video, audio, lip sync, upscaling, and mobile editing. ### 1.4 Safety research and capability forecasts remain conditional (→)

Two videos address different kinds of uncertainty: one summarizes a research paper on model supply-chain risk, while the other reports a forecast about future AI research capabilities.

[Internet of Bugs](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCfJRkBBiPtKZyZn_3PxWaiQ) discusses the paper [“Understanding and Enhancing Backdoor Persistency in LLM Agent Post-Training”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07510). The paper studies whether hidden behavior can persist through benign post-training and reports persistence in some experimental conditions. It identifies a supply-chain risk, not the prevalence of such behavior in deployed systems. ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rloMMrPNDPU))

## 2. What Frustrates People

The sample is mostly tutorials, vendor presentations, interviews, and news rather than direct customer reports. Comment text is unavailable, so prevalence and audience agreement cannot be measured.

### Multi-shot video can miss intended camera movement

**Severity: Medium; prevalence: one creator-described workflow.** Artlist's video describes generated shots with misplaced cars or incorrect camera direction and presents Blender scene blocking as a way to guide timing and camera movement. The description provides no comparative failure rate or independent test. ([Artlist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b5RI-tdtyE0))

### “Free” video-generation access has conditions

**Severity: Medium for tool selection; prevalence: unknown.** Malva AI's tutorial says that access may depend on promotional credits, ads, or eligibility, and that features, regional access, and limits may change. The video is sponsored and reflects one creator's tests, not a survey of users or a verified comparison of current terms. ([Malva AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mNsR_KfjZpY))

### Agent model provenance is a security concern in research

**Severity: Potentially high if applicable; prevalence: not measured.** The cited research studies whether hidden model behavior can persist through benign post-training. The accompanying video recommends known model sources, least privilege, monitoring, and cross-checking, but the snapshot does not report real-world incidents or measure those controls' effectiveness. ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07510), [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rloMMrPNDPU))
