## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 AI hardware and data-center literacy (🡕)

One high-reach explainer takes viewers from an AI prompt through a data center, memory, wiring, transistors, and atoms. The video uses NVIDIA’s Rubin GPU as an example, but explicitly cautions that its chip interiors are illustrative 3D models, not a Rubin blueprint, and that Rubin dimensions are not public. NVIDIA’s linked platform article frames Vera Rubin as a co-designed system of chips, networking, software, power, and cooling; that is the vendor’s own description. This is one video, with 847,131 views, 7,175 likes, and 826 comments as engagement.

[Silicon Up Close](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCzKqZDfE1KvWKPxtqHyMBiQ) published [*What Happens Inside an AI Chip When You Ask a Question*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oBKrR3i6jWM), an explainer that follows the hardware path while distinguishing illustrative visuals from disclosed specifications.
