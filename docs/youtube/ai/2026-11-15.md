## 2. What Frustrates People

The sample is mainly creator tutorials, news, and panel discussions rather than first-person customer reports. It supports a few recurring workflow and deployment constraints, but not estimates of how widespread they are.

### Keeping characters and edits consistent costs iteration

**Severity: Medium, based on creator-described workflows.** Jack Vs. AI's tutorials emphasize reference sheets, anchor references, and fixing generation mistakes without repeated expensive renders. Malva AI also demonstrates clip corrections and character consistency. These examples show where creators spend effort, but do not measure error rates or the number of users affected. ([Jack Vs. AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EXD_DeCRdhc), [animation tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_X7KQVwsn9U), [Malva AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axBF6zXzsNA))

### Image models still miss some precision tasks

**Severity: Medium in the creator's test suite; broader prevalence unknown.** AI Samson reports remaining anatomy and proportion errors in its comparison of ChatGPT Images 2.6 and 2.5. The linked test page also reports mixed results across difficult prompts, including some worse results in 2.6. This is creator-run testing, not a standardized evaluation of users' work. ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRp2rVC0nEE), [comparison](https://ltx.aifilmmaker.academy/gpt-image-2-6-sunburst/))

### Access conditions complicate “free” tool claims

**Severity: Low to Medium for planning.** Malva AI says its “free” and “unlimited” experience may vary by account or region and that limits, features, verification, and pricing can change. That is a creator's disclosure, not evidence that users broadly encounter a specific failure or cost. ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=axBF6zXzsNA))

### Robotics deployments lack the measurements needed to judge readiness

**Severity: Medium as an adoption concern raised in the panel.** The medical-robotics panel names safety, clinical workflows, regulation, and reimbursement as barriers. The AI News roundup says Xiaomi called an eight-hour factory shift early validation but had not shared its full-shift success rate. Neither item supplies independent task-level reliability results. ([panel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BYH1L7xAGCw), [AI News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vu-_JAIpqGE))
