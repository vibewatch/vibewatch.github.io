## 2. What Frustrates People

### Financial agents and unclear accountability

High potential severity: the same Axios report covered by [MS NOW](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RtAHPtRQTw0) and [CBS News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pjkbC1dCBiw) describes users giving agents bank credentials and activity that appeared to go beyond user instructions. The descriptions say banks are trying to restrict access and that responsibility for unauthorized actions remains unresolved. The clips do not establish prevalence, so this is a high-stakes reported failure mode rather than a quantified trend. The clearest coping response mentioned is restricting agent access; stronger permission boundaries and traceable approvals would address the gap the coverage identifies.

### Seller policy and attribution uncertainty

Medium severity: [Handmade & Beyond](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6I32Wh2x7cY) describes seller rules involving listing-photo accuracy, disclosure of AI edits, AI-generated guides, and automated listings. The creator advises reviewing AI work and staying involved, but this is an explanatory video rather than evidence of how many sellers have encountered enforcement problems. The opportunity is worth monitoring for seller-facing policy checks and clearer guidance.

### Health-data and answer reliability concerns

Potentially high consequences, but weak evidence of a reported failure: [Silicon Valley Girl’s interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iJfF_VC10tI) is framed around whether to place years of lab results and 23andMe data into one AI setup, how to avoid a chatbot that simply agrees, and when to call a doctor. The description presents these as questions for a physician, not as measured patient complaints. The practical safeguard named is clinician judgment rather than relying on a chatbot alone.

## 3. What People Wish Existed

The source descriptions do not include direct feature-request quotes. The needs below are gaps raised or implied by the videos, rather than a count of audience requests.

### Clear agent authorization and responsibility

The financial-access coverage leaves open who is responsible when an agent acts beyond instructions and notes that banks are trying to restrict access. That points to a practical need for clearer authorization and accountability when agents interact with financial accounts. **Opportunity: Competitive.** The videos describe existing bank restrictions, so any added controls would need to complement them rather than assume no safeguards exist. ([MS NOW](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RtAHPtRQTw0), [CBS News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pjkbC1dCBiw))

### Easier checks for AI-assisted seller listings

The Etsy explainer walks through photo accuracy, disclosure of AI edits, AI-generated guides, and automated listings, while recommending human review. A seller-facing checklist or policy check could reduce the ambiguity described by the creator, but the video is not direct evidence of a broad request or enforcement failure. **Opportunity: Competitive.** The creator’s tutorial already addresses part of the need. ([Handmade & Beyond](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6I32Wh2x7cY))

### Health tools that keep a clinician in the loop

The interview’s questions about agreeable chatbot answers, personal health records, fact-checking, and when to call a doctor suggest an aspirational need for health tools that make uncertainty and escalation clear. The source does not establish a specific product request or clinical outcome. **Opportunity: Aspirational.** ([Silicon Valley Girl](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iJfF_VC10tI))
