## 1. What People Are Talking About

### 1.1 Agent safety, understanding, and governance (→)

## 2. What Frustrates People

### Agent reliability and visibility

High salience in the videos, but not a measured rate of user frustration. [CNN](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OnXVUXktKxQ) describes developers’ incomplete understanding of frontier models, while [Maddy Zhang](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VioTvuDS23k) says long-running agent workflows need stop conditions and verification and can accrue “comprehension debt.” [DW News](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H9SvA0pkuvs) adds concerns about incompatible rules across jurisdictions. These sources identify risks and engineering problems, but do not quantify incidents or establish willingness to pay. Better evaluation, logging, and human override are plausible areas to explore; market demand is not demonstrated here.

### Inference cost and access

Medium evidence of practical infrastructure constraints: the [AI infrastructure project guide](https://www.tech5ense.com/p/5-ai-infrastructure-projects-to-build-in-2026) covers KV-cache use, cold starts, inference metrics, and cost trade-offs, and says most projects can be done without GPU access. DeepSeek’s [DeepInfer repository](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepInfer) documents model and hardware limits and notes that a KV-cache system and load balancing are not included in the release. These are concrete deployment gaps and workarounds, not first-hand reports of failed deployments.

### Free-tier and platform-policy uncertainty

Medium evidence from creator tutorials. [Malva AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoFeT_SJXvg) warns that “free” video-generation modes may require credits or payment and that pricing, watermark rules, and availability can change. [Handmade & Beyond](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6I32Wh2x7cY) describes seller-side requirements around photo accuracy, disclosure, and human involvement. Comparing tools and reviewing generated listings are the coping methods described; neither video provides direct audience requests for a compliance product.

## 7. Where the Opportunities Are

**[++] Reliable controls for long-running agents** — CNN describes limits in understanding frontier models, while Maddy Zhang’s video emphasizes stop conditions, verification, and comprehension debt. A bounded-execution and audit layer is supported by repeated technical and policy attention, though direct buyer demand is not shown.

**[++] Cost-aware inference and model deployment** — DeepSeek’s release spans benchmark claims, Search/Fetch APIs, and an inference runtime; Vishakha Sadhwani’s project guide covers KV cache, cost trade-offs, GPU access, and Kubernetes serving. The opportunity is in making model choice, caching, routing, and hardware requirements easier to test and operate.

**[+] Creator compliance and predictable access** — Malva AI’s tutorial explicitly qualifies changing credit, pricing, and watermark terms, while Handmade & Beyond discusses disclosure and human involvement for Etsy listings. A tool that tracks generation terms and platform-specific review steps is an emerging opportunity, not a stated request from creators in this evidence.
